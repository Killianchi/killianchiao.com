Killian Chiao

Born on the Arctic Circle in the Norwegian city of Mo i Rana to a Norwegian state conflict mediator mother who worked with terrorists and criminals, and a Swiss-French father in investment banking. Killian grew up surrounded by high-stakes thinking and developed an early obsession with the board game Monopoly.

Time is ticking in one direction, focusing on experiences that can anchor time and place to create solid memories is what matters. Delaying instant gratification, learning from and enabling people and organisations to build products and experience to change minds is what counts and pays off long term.
